Cooling repairs for North Central WV homes

Summers around Morgantown, Fairmont, and Bridgeport get humid, and a lot of houses rely on a single outdoor unit to keep the whole home comfortable. This page is for homeowners who need air conditioning repair, whether the system will not start, the air feels warm, or ice is building up on the equipment.

Request a quote if you hear unusual noises, notice weak airflow, or the thermostat is calling for cool and nothing happens. Photos of the outdoor unit and the thermostat can help the first follow-up go faster.

What to expect

The technician should look at the system, talk through what they find, and give you a price before they repair or replace parts. Ask what they think is going on and whether a repair still makes sense for the age of the unit.
