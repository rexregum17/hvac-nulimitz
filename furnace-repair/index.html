Heat when the hills get cold

Winters in North Central West Virginia can drop fast, and many homes still rely on a gas or electric furnace. This page is for no-heat calls, furnaces that start and stop, or air that blows but never feels warm.

When you request a quote, mention any smell, unusual sounds, or a pilot or ignition that will not stay lit. If the house is already cold, say that up front so the follow-up can be prioritized.

What to expect

A technician should inspect the furnace, explain what they see, and give you a price before the repair begins. Ask about safety checks as well as the repair itself, and ask about licensing and insurance before you book.

If the furnace is worn out, you can also request a quote for a new system on the installation page. Some homes are a better fit for a heat pump. That can be part of the same conversation.
